# Hackathon Notes

## SkyCastle Studio AI Hackathon — September 2026

**Project:** VIKO ASHURA — *The King Who Chose to Forget*  
**Category:** MainCharacter  
**Result:** 🏆 **Grand Prize**  
**Prize:** **$300**

## Submission

The final build combined several parts into one character experience:

- a 15-second cinematic Studio video
- AI-generated character and story imagery
- an interactive VibeFlow website
- a one-time choose-your-fate decision tied directly to Viko's central conflict

### Tools

- **ByteDance · Seedance 2.5** — cinematic video
- **xAI · Grok Imagine Image 2.0** — character / story imagery
- **VibeFlow** — interactive story experience

## Original submission copy

> Viko erased the King he used to be. Now the Queen he chose to forget returns with one truth: humanity may survive only if he becomes that King again.
>
> He didn't just forget a throne.
>
> **He forgot the woman who ruled beside him.**

The experience ends with the challenge:

**Would you remember?**

*Choose once. Accept the consequence.*

## Why the project stood out

The official winner announcement highlighted that the project did not stop at an image and a video. It combined the Studio media workflow with VibeFlow to create an interactive website where the character's central dilemma becomes a decision for the audience.

The announcement described the combination as **"Studio + VibeFlow"** and specifically called out the one-choice consequence mechanic.
