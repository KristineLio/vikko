# Story — VIKO ASHURA

## The King Who Chose to Forget

Viko Ashura erased the King he used to be.

He did not lose his memory by accident. Forgetting was a choice: a deliberate break from the throne, the violence, the responsibility, and the identity that came with his former life.

But the past returns through Kleopatra—the Queen who remembers the life they shared even though Viko does not.

She brings a truth he cannot ignore:

**humanity may survive only if Viko becomes that King again.**

The conflict is not simply whether Viko can remember. It is whether he should.

## Central choice

The interactive experience asks the player to decide Viko's fate.

The choice is designed as part of the character conflict itself rather than an optional interaction layered on top of a finished story.

**Would you remember?**

Choose once. Accept the consequence.

## Character contrast

Viko's identity is built around two states:

- **Fragmented past** — the erased King, royal duty, power, war, Kleopatra, and the person he rejected.
- **Chosen present** — the man Viko deliberately became after removing that identity from himself.

Kleopatra exists at the collision point between them. She remembers what Viko chose not to.

## Visual language

Viko's design uses:

- black tousled hair
- amber-gold eyes
- a black coat with aged-gold Ashura detailing
- a three-point broken-crown emblem
- segmented black-and-gold Karma / halo elements
- dark steel weaponry

The visual system avoids clean royal grandeur. The gold is aged and fragmented, reflecting authority that still exists but has been deliberately broken apart.

## Core premise

> What if the strongest thing a king ever did was erase himself—and the world eventually needed that king back?
